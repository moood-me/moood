# The README's diagram: how moood fits together (2026-10-30)

**Why.** Pavel liked the diagram drawn for the story «Make your own game» (moood-me/stories) and asked for it on the
repository's front page, in every language the READMEs have.

**What.** `docs/images/how-it-fits.<lang>.svg` for the twelve languages — the author with their agent, using moood and
the open works repositories, writing into their own repository; where a game is played (on one's own machine, on one's
own site or app, through the studio on moood.me); the studio's services (render, measuring, speech, music, motion, the
artist), which an agent asks for through its MCP or API. Plain SVG, text as text (the system's fonts), the canvas its
own light background (it reads the same in GitHub's dark theme). Each `README.<lang>.md` shows its language's diagram
under the opening paragraphs.

**How.** One template, the words per language (the READMEs' own terms and address — «tú» in Spanish, ワールド, 월드);
each drawn in Chrome and checked that no line of text leaves its box.
