# A game's fonts are its own (2026-10-29)

**Why.** On the new moood.me the Fall of the House of Usher stopped at once: «no stylesheet ui.css». Its `ui.css` (and
Scarlet Sails', Cinema Paradiso's) began with `@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/…")`; the new site's pages
allow styles and fonts only from their own address (CSP `style-src 'self'`, `font-src 'self' data:`), the import was
blocked, the stylesheet failed and the runtime stopped the game. The old site allowed Google Fonts. Pavel: fix it
properly — in the games, the fonts carried by them (done in works-003, works-005: their history notes) — and tell the
authors.

**What.** `docs/games/README.md`: everything a game or a scene loads is its own — no fonts, scripts, styles or
pictures from another address; a font in `bespoke/` (woff2, or `data:` in `@font-face`), free-licensed, with the
subsets of the game's languages.
